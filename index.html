A novelist who kept building tools.

It forgets — by chapter 3 it has lost the thread planted in chapter 1. It drifts — metadata tags and progress-bar characters leak into the prose. It slips — a story set today suddenly says “the year 2034.” I didn’t abandon the book. I turned every one of those failures into a tool. Finishing is rarer than writing well.
